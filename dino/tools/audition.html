Audition the sound bank

Every row is a recorded file, not a synthesised beep. The button plays one of the variants the games actually ask for, at random, so repeated taps show why four copies of the same hit are better than one. The numbers beside it are what the renderer measured off the finished file — peak, loudness, crest and, for a loop, how flat the join is — so a claim about the sound can be checked here instead of taken on trust. Nothing plays until you press something.
