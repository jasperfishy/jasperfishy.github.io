Guardian privacy notice

This draft describes Roader Guardian’s operational data. It must be completed by the responsible organisation before publication.

Who is responsible

Southern Taxis Ltd is responsible for the taxi operational records described in this notice.

Address: 98 Warren Road, Brighton BN2 6BA.
Privacy enquiries and requests: enquiries@southerntaxis.co.uk.

To confirm: the role of JasperFish as developer or service provider.

Technical contact: jasperfish@gmail.com.

Information processed

Guardian processes Google account email addresses and agent names; driver identifiers; queue, priority, suspension and allocation records; no-fare and cancellation history; timestamps; and audit records. Backups may contain copies of workbook data.

Why information is used

The system supports taxi operations, records actions, checks reliability, investigates faults and provides management reports.

To confirm: the organisation’s lawful basis for each purpose, any applicable legitimate interests, and whether supplying each category of information is required.

Access and service providers

Authorised operators, administrators and designated management can access records according to their permissions. The system uses Google Sheets, Drive and Apps Script. Healthchecks receives availability pings. Authorised maintenance may also involve administrator and AI tools, including access to operational records when needed for the requested work.

To confirm: the complete recipient and service-provider list, provider roles, and any international transfers and safeguards.

How long information is kept

Operational history, audit records and backups can remain after a queue entry changes. There is currently no verified, comprehensive automatic deletion schedule.

Southern Taxis has approved target retention periods of 12 months for operational history and audit records, and 90 days for routine diagnostic logs and backups, subject to documented investigations and applicable record-keeping requirements. These targets are awaiting implementation and verification; they are not a statement that automatic deletion currently occurs. Existing records and copies may remain beyond these targets during this transition.

To complete before publication: document the retention justification, verify the disposal and backup process, specify the transition arrangements and explain how deletion requests are handled.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law provides rights to access, correct or erase information, restrict processing, object to processing and receive portable data. Some rights depend on the lawful basis and applicable exceptions. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

To confirm: whether any processing relies on consent, and whether automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects occur. Complete the required explanation if they do.
