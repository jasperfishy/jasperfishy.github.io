1. What this application is

Roader Guardian is a private automation and reliability-monitoring tool operated solely by its owner. It is not a public service and is not offered to, or intended for use by, any other person. It exists to monitor and maintain the owner's own spreadsheet-based operational systems.

2. Data we access

To perform its function, the application requests access to the owner's own Google account via Google OAuth, limited to the following scopes:

  • Google Sheets — to read and update the owner's operational and administration spreadsheets;
  • Google Drive — to read and store the owner's backup and configuration files;
  • Google Apps Script — to read and run the owner's own controller scripts.

All data accessed belongs to the owner. The application does not access any third party's data.

3. How data is used

Accessed data is used only to run the owner's reliability checks, write heartbeat and status records, and keep the owner's spreadsheets operating. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics resale, or any purpose unrelated to the owner's own systems.

4. Storage and sharing

Data remains within the owner's Google account and the owner's own machines. We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal data to any third party. No data is transferred to advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Data retention

Operational records are retained in the owner's spreadsheets and backups for as long as the owner chooses. The owner may delete them at any time. OAuth access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.

6. Security

OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's own device and are never published or shared. Access is restricted to the owner.

7. Children

This application is a private operational tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will be revised accordingly.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, contact jasperfish@gmail.com.
