REAL DINOSAUR PICTURES AND SHORT VIDEOS FOR DINO QUEST
=======================================================
Drop licence-safe files in THIS folder, then list them in manifest.json. Until you do,
the game shows a friendly illustrated "Dino fact" card instead, so nothing is broken.

Three slots (the "id" must match):
  mountain  - shown at the top of Mount Roar   (a fossil footprint trail, or a real mountain landscape)
  cave      - shown inside the cave            (a fossil dinosaur egg or nest)
  hatch     - shown when the baby hatches      (a fossil baby dinosaur, or a hatching bird chick)

manifest.json example:
{ "moments": [
  { "id": "cave", "type": "image", "src": "egg-nest.jpg", "caption": "A real fossil dinosaur nest with eggs.", "credit": "Photo: Name, CC BY 4.0" },
  { "id": "hatch", "type": "video", "src": "hatch.mp4", "caption": "A baby bird hatching, just like dinosaurs did!", "credit": "Video: Name, CC0" }
] }

WHAT TO GET AND WHERE (check each item's licence page and keep the credit text):
  - Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org): search "dinosaur egg fossil", "dinosaur footprints", "Protoceratops" -
    filter to Public Domain or CC0, or CC BY (then fill in "credit").
  - Smithsonian Open Access (si.edu/openaccess) and the American Museum of Natural History Library: CC0 images.
  - NASA / USGS / NPS (nps.gov, usgs.gov): public-domain fossil and trackway photos.
  - Pixabay and Pexels: free-licence short videos (search "chick hatching", "bird nest", "cave", "mountain").
  - Avoid: movie stills, toy photos, anything marked "all rights reserved", and CC BY-NC.
FILE TIPS: JPG under 400 KB, MP4 (H.264) under 3 MB and under 10 seconds, no sound needed.
