Southern Taxis Roader Guardian Deployment

Roader Guardian supports authorised taxi operators and agents who manage driver queues and operational records in Google Sheets.

What Guardian does

  • Supports driver queue and allocation workflows.
  • Records operational actions and agent choices for auditing.
  • Checks system health and flags issues for management.

Access

Guardian operates within the organisation’s Google workbooks. This information page does not provide access to driver records. Access is granted by the organisation’s administrator.

Google account permissions

Deployment and maintenance use Google account authorisation for the Google services required by the configured system, including Sheets, Drive and Apps Script. Ask the administrator if you are unsure why a permission is requested.

Help

For technical support, contact jasperfish@gmail.com. For operational decisions and record corrections, contact your taxi organisation’s manager.
