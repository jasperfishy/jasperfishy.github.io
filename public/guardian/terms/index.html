Guardian terms of use

This is a draft of operational use terms. The responsible organisation must confirm the provider identity and final terms before publication.

Authorised use

Use Guardian only if the taxi organisation has authorised your access. Follow its procedures for queue changes, allocations, agent selection and record corrections.

Account security

Use your authorised account and keep its credentials secure. Report unexpected access or permission requests to your administrator.

Accurate records

Check the driver and action before making a change. Select the correct agent when requested. Report mistakes to management so that corrections preserve the audit history.

System faults

If a check fails or a queue behaves unexpectedly, contact the administrator and follow the organisation’s agreed fallback procedure. Confirm the outcome of an interrupted action before repeating it.

Privacy

Read the Guardian privacy notice for information about operational records and access.
